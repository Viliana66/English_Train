How to use this: the Drill cards are for learning. This sheet is for quickly looking something up.

The Bulgarian meanings and source grouping come from the material you supplied. Where an English pattern needs an object or a more natural construction, the English pattern column makes that explicit. A few special cases are marked with a note.
